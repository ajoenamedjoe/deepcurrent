How a score is built. Five independent signals each resolve to −1…+1 and are weighted: options flow 30 (net directional premium and ask-side conviction), open-interest build 20 (positions actually opened, not scalped), dark pool 15 (block premium vs the day's notional, leaning by print vs NBBO mid), gamma 15 (spot vs flip, room to the call/put wall), technicals 20 (trend stack, RSI, pullback-in-trend). Haircuts apply for earnings inside the horizon, IV rank above 85, and thin relative volume. A setup is only surfaced when at least three signals agree in direction and reward:risk clears 1.3.

This is descriptive aggregation of market data, not trade advice or a recommendation. Levels are arithmetic from ATR and gamma walls, not predictions. Verify everything against your own broker data before risking capital.
