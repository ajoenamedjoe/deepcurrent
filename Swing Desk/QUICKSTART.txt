UW SWING DESK - QUICK START
===========================

EVERY TIME YOU WANT TO USE IT:

  1. Double-click  START_HERE.bat
  2. Wait a few seconds - your browser opens by itself
  3. Leave the black window open while you use it
     (you can minimize it - just don't close it)

The first time only, it asks you to paste your Unusual Whales
API token. After that it remembers.

Best hours: start it around 9:45am ET, trust it from 10:00,
close it after 4:00pm ET. Outside market hours the data is
frozen on the last session.


READING THE ALERTS
------------------
Each card is a setup where at least 3 of 5 signals agree.
Seeing none is normal - it stays quiet unless things line up.

The badge that matters most is the green one:
     HELD 7 SCANS - 7/7
A setup that has held across many scans is real. One that
says "just appeared" may be gone in a minute. Persistence is
the signal; a single flash is noise.

Tick "Show below-threshold watchlist" to see near-misses and
why each one is held back.


DISCORD ALERTS (optional)
-------------------------
Your own private channel only: posting Unusual Whales data to other people
(a public or paid server) may need a redistribution licence from Unusual Whales.

To get posts in Discord:

  1. In Discord, right-click your channel
  2. Edit Channel -> Integrations -> Webhooks -> New Webhook
  3. Click "Copy Webhook URL"
  4. Open the file named  .env  in Notepad
  5. Add a line:   DISCORD_WEBHOOK_URL=<paste the URL here>
  6. Save, close the black window, run START_HERE.bat again

To test it, visit:  http://127.0.0.1:8787/api/notify/test

You'll only get posts for HIGH-CONVICTION setups: score 60+,
4 of 5 signals agreeing, and held for 3 scans in a row. Plus
one summary each weekday at 4:00pm ET.

This is deliberately quiet. Expect a handful a week, not a
handful an hour.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
Browser didn't open   ->  go to http://127.0.0.1:8787
Blue "protected your PC" box -> More info, then Run anyway
"Python isn't installed" -> https://www.python.org/downloads/
                            tick "Add python.exe to PATH"
To change your token  ->  delete .env, run START_HERE.bat again

Full details are in README.md
